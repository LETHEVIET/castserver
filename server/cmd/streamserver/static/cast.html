How it works

  1. Click Share Screen and pick a tab, window, or your entire screen.
  2. The browser captures video and sends it to the server via WebSocket.
  3. The server converts it to 256×192 YUV and sends it to the 3DS over UDP.
  4. Your 3DS displays the stream in real-time.

For best performance, choose a single browser tab rather than the entire screen.
